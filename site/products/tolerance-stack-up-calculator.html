What's inside

Check a gap before you build. Enter each dimension of the loop (sign, nominal, upper and lower deviations) and the workbook gives you:

  • the nominal gap J and its worst-case range (100 % of assemblies);
  • the RSS range (root sum square, ±3σ, about 99.73 % of assemblies);
  • a pass / fail check against your min and max requirement;
  • the weight of each dimension in the variation, with a bar chart, to see which tolerance to tighten first.

What you get

  • English and French versions (.xlsx), up to 30 dimensions per stack.
  • Worked example preloaded (housing + two parts), plus a "How to use" tab with the method and formulas.
  • Formulas only, no macros: standard Excel functions.

The formulas and the example are explained free of charge at baouch.fr. This workbook is the offline, multi-line version for your own projects.

Linear (1D) stacks only. Provided as is: check the results on your own cases.
