What's inside

When can you actually retire early? Type 8 numbers into the yellow cells and this spreadsheet tells you your FIRE number, how many years you have left, and the age you reach financial independence.

What you get (1 Excel file, 5 tabs)

  • Start Here: 3-minute instructions, legend and assumptions in plain English.
  • FIRE Calculator: FIRE number (4% rule) + a conservative 3.5% version, % progress, years and age to financial independence, Coast FIRE number, and the yearly/monthly savings needed to retire at your target age.
  • Year by Year: 60-year projection of your portfolio. Rows turn green the year you are financially independent.
  • Savings Rate Table: enter your take-home pay and see years-to-FI at every savings rate from 5% to 90%.
  • Sources: Bengen (1994), the Trinity study (1998), SEC Investor.gov and BLS CPI, plus every formula explained.
