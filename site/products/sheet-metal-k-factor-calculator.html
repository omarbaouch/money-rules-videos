What's inside

Cut the right blank length the first time.

  • Flat length: up to 20 flanges and bends, outside dimensions, one radius per bend if needed; bend allowance (BA), outside setback (OSSB) and bend deduction (BD) for every bend.
  • K-factor measurement: bend a test strip, enter the strip length and the two measured flanges, get your shop's real K-factor.
  • Bend deduction chart: BD for radii 0.5 to 10 mm and angles 30° to 135°, recalculated for your thickness and K-factor.

  • English and French versions (.xlsx), with a preloaded example: 2 mm sheet, 90° bend → 76.40 mm flat length.
  • "How to use" tab with the formulas and conventions (bend angle vs included angle, outside dimensions).
  • Formulas only, no macros: standard Excel functions.
