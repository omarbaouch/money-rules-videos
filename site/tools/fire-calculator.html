FIRE calculator

Your FIRE number is your yearly spending divided by a safe withdrawal rate (4% = 25× spending). All amounts in today's dollars.

Assumes a constant real (after-inflation) return and contributions at the end of each year. The ~4% rule comes from Bengen (1994) and the Trinity study (1998) on 30-year retirements. Educational only, not financial advice.
